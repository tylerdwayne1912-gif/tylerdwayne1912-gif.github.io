📱 Install on Android

  1. Tap Download APK below.
  2. If prompted, allow installs from this source in Settings.
  3. Open the downloaded NextTrade.apk file.
  4. Tap Install and open the app.

🍎 Install on iPhone / iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari (required by iOS).
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (box with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add — the NextTrade icon appears on your home screen.
